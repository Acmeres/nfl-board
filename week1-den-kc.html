What is not validated

excluded Anytime touchdown — cannot be de-vigged from a Yes-only feed without an external estimate of the board total.

unproven Prop profitability — not backtestable at all; forward closing-line value is the only evidence this model can ever produce.

wrong Game-level correlations — roughly three times too strong on blowout-versus-total and the wrong sign on the two team scores, an artefact of a fixed clock conserving drives. Do not price a same-game parlay off this.
