What this page is

Who wins each game, and why — not what to bet. There is no price, no EV and no book anywhere on it. Walk-forward over 2018–2025 the model calls winners at about 62% and scores 0.6364 log loss against a coin flip’s 0.6931, so it knows real things about football. It also does not beat the closing line, which is a separate claim and lives on the picks board.

Each unit row is one side’s offence against the other’s defence in EPA per play, measured as deviations from the league average and added — a good offence and a bad defence compound, they do not cancel. 8 of 32 teams start a passer other than the one who threw most of 2025, so their ratings describe an offence that will not take the field. Those are flagged on the game.

Injuries come from Sleeper, not from nflverse. nflverse publishes injury reports only once a season is underway, so there are none for 2026 yet. Sleeper is free, current, and lists 88 injured starters across this slate. It is also a snapshot with no history behind it, which is the important limit: it is shown here because it is useful to see, and it is deliberately NOT fed into the model, because a coefficient cannot be fitted on a feed with no past. When nflverse publishes, the model switches to it — that version is time-cuttable and was measured to help (log loss 0.6437 → 0.6408).
